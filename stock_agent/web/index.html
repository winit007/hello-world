    How to use

    Three steps: scan in the evening, check in the morning, manage the exit.

    1. Evening: scan

    1. After the market closes (3:30 pm IST), open Today's picks and press Scan now.
    2. Each card is one idea. CALL expects the stock to rise, PUT expects a fall.
    3. Past trades won is how often this exact trade (buy next morning, stop, target, exit date) made money after this setup on this stock. The grey tick is the same trade on an ordinary day; the gap is the edge.

    2. Morning: check and buy

    1. Skip cards that say Not affordable or Edge faded.
    2. Look up the contract in your broker app. The premium here is an estimate; if the live one is much higher, the trade is riskier than planned.
    3. Buy only the number of lots shown, then press Add to my trades with your real price.

    3. Manage the exit

    1. Set price alerts on the stock at Stop, Target 1 and Target 2.
    2. Stock trades through the stop: sell everything at once, don't wait for the close. Target 1: sell half. Target 2: sell the rest.
    3. Nothing hit by Exit by: sell anyway, because options lose value daily.
    4. Close the trade in My trades to track your win rate.

    The other tabs

    1. Crypto and Commodities use the same candlestick engine on coins (daily) and gold, silver, crude, gas and copper (15-minute). Each shows its own strategy check at the top.
    2. Goals turns a target amount and date into a monthly investment and a mix of equity, debt and gold, with the chance of getting there.
    3. Rebalance compares what you own with that mix and lists what to buy or sell, or how to place new money so nothing has to be sold.
    4. Phone app, no PC needed: open winit007.github.io/hello-world in Chrome and tap Install app. It shows the evening run's results; Goals, Rebalance and My trades work on the phone.
    5. This PC app on your phone: easiest is the Stock Agent (anywhere) launcher (works on mobile data too); then scan the QR code in Settings and Add to Home screen. Details are in Settings.

    My screener: what each criterion means

    A stock is kept only if every rule is true. The survivors are sorted by Rank by and the top ones are held in equal money. Measures marked % are typed as percentages: 20 means 20%, -5 means 5% below.

    The other choices

    StocksWhich list is checked: Nifty 50 (the 50 largest companies), Nifty 100, Nifty 200, or the F&O stocks (those with futures and options). A bigger list gives more choice but includes smaller, more volatile companies.
    Start fromFills in a ready-made rule set (below) that you can then change. My own rules keeps what you typed.
    Rank by / OrderAfter the rules, the passing stocks are sorted by this measure. Use highest first for returns (strongest stocks on top) and lowest first for volatility or beta (calmest on top). For Worst fall and Distance from 52-week high, which are negative numbers, highest first means the smallest fall.
    HoldHow many of the top-ranked stocks you buy. Your capital is split equally: ₹2,00,000 and 10 stocks means about ₹20,000 each. Fewer stocks means bigger swings; 10 to 20 is usual.
    RebalanceHow often the rules are run again and the holdings swapped for the new top list. Monthly follows changes fastest but trades and pays tax more often; yearly trades least and defers tax (gains held over a year are taxed at 12.5% instead of 20%).

    The ready-made rule sets

    Momentum leadersPrice above its 200-day average (a long-term uptrend) and over ₹20 crore traded a day (easy to buy and sell), ranked by the best 6-month return. The idea behind momentum funds: stocks that have been rising tend to keep rising for a while.
    Near 52-week highWithin 5% of the highest price of the past year and up more than 20% over the year, ranked by 3-month return. Strong stocks about to break out to new highs.
    Pullback in an uptrendAbove the 200-day average but RSI below 40: a strong stock having a short dip. Ranked by 12-month return, so the strongest dipping stocks come first.
    Low volatilityBeta below 0.9 (moves less than the Nifty), ranked by lowest volatility. Smoother ride and smaller falls, usually with somewhat lower returns in strong markets.

    Reading the results

    Your screen, a yearAverage yearly growth (CAGR) if you had followed these rules over the backtest, after 0.25% trading cost, before tax.
    Nifty 50, a yearThe same for a Nifty 50 index fund, for comparison.
    All these stocks, equal weightBuying every stock in the list in equal money. The list is today's index members (the companies that survived), so this number is flattered. If your screen does not beat it, your rules are not adding anything.
    Worst fallThe biggest drop from a peak to a later low. Ask yourself whether you would have held on through it.
    Picks that roseThe share of picks that were higher at the next rebalance, and the share that beat the Nifty over the same period.
    Trading a yearTurnover: 100% means the whole portfolio was replaced once a year. More trading means more costs and more tax.

    Trade checklist

    Take a trade only if every answer is yes. Each pick shows these; live broker orders are refused until all pass.

    1. Proven edge: has this setup made money on data it wasn't tuned on, after costs?
    2. Planned exit: stop-loss, target and time limit known before entering?
    3. Small risk: loss at the stop within 2% of capital (₹4,000 for ₹2,00,000)?
    4. Liquid contract: bid-ask spread under about 2% of the price?
    5. No big events: no results, RBI decision or budget before your exit?
    6. Intraday timing: not in the first 15 minutes after 9:15, and out before the 3:20 pm square-off?

    The full app on your Android phone (no PC)

    Runs the complete app on the phone itself: live scans, stock lookup, paper trading and broker orders. Nothing is uploaded; it only answers this phone.

    1. Install Termux from F-Droid or GitHub (the old Play Store version does not work).
    2. Open Termux, paste this and press Enter. It takes 5 to 10 minutes the first time:
      curl -fsSL https://winit007.github.io/hello-world/termux.sh | bash
    3. Then type stock-agent. The app opens in Chrome at 127.0.0.1:8765. Keep Termux running in the background while you use it.
    4. Home-screen icon: install Termux:Widget and add its widget, then tap Stock Agent. Set Termux's battery use to Unrestricted so Android does not stop it.
    5. Kite: use the same redirect URL as on the PC, http://127.0.0.1:8765/kite/callback, and enter your API key in Settings on the phone.

    Paper trading

    1. Open Paper trading. You start with virtual money equal to your capital in Settings; Reset account starts again with any amount.
    2. Options can be bought or sold (written). Selling blocks about 18% of the contract value as margin, earns at most the premium and can lose much more, so practise it here before trying it for real.
    3. On any pick, lookup, crypto or commodity card press Paper trade to copy its lots, stop, Target 1 and exit date into the order form, then Preview and Place order.
    4. The tab refreshes every minute while open: stops and targets close positions automatically, intraday trades square off at 3:20 pm, and charges are deducted like a real broker.
    5. After 20 or more trades, check the win rate and total P&L before risking real money.

    Stay safe

    1. Take one or two picks, not all five. They often move together.
    2. A 60% setup still loses 4 times in 10. The stop keeps each loss near your risk limit.
    3. The Strategy check banner shows how these rules did on recent years they were not tuned on. While it is negative, use the picks for research and practice mode only.
    4. This is a statistics tool, not investment advice.
