Rules used

Vaastu placement
    Ventilation
    • Every habitable room and the kitchen needs a window on a wall that is open to outside air.
    • Window area must be at least the configured share of the floor area (default 10%).
    • Windows on two different exterior walls earn credit for cross ventilation.
    • Toilets need an exterior window, or a mechanical exhaust as a fallback.
    • The flat should have at least two open sides.
    Layout
    • Rooms must stay inside the plot and must not overlap.
    • Minimum areas: living 120, master bedroom 120, bedroom 100, study 60, kitchen 50, toilet 18 sq ft.
    • A plan needs a living room, a kitchen, a bedroom, a toilet and a main entrance.
    • At least 5% of the plot should remain for circulation (passages count).
