    Advanced Dental Technology at Our Hospital

    We invest in modern equipment so that diagnosis is more accurate, treatment is faster and your visit is more comfortable.

    Instant, high-resolution digital X-rays that use a fraction of the radiation of traditional film. Images appear in seconds, for faster and more accurate diagnosis.

    RVG Digital X-Ray – Tooth Scan

    A small digital sensor captures a detailed image of one or a few teeth in seconds, right at the chair: ideal for checking decay, infection and root canals.

    Intraoral Camera – See What We See

    A high-definition camera shows you live images of your teeth on screen, so you understand your diagnosis and can plan treatment together with Dr. Wagisha.

    Rotary RCT System – Faster, Painless Root Canals

    An advanced rotary endodontic system completes most root canals in a single visit, with less discomfort, shorter appointments and greater precision.

    Endo Activator – Deeper Canal Cleaning

    Gentle sonic vibrations help disinfecting solutions reach every corner of the root canal, for cleaner canals and better long-term results.

    3D Obturation Unit – Complete Canal Sealing

    Warm, three-dimensional filling seals the entire root canal system, helping prevent re-infection and protecting the tooth for years.

    Patient Safety & Infection Control

    Every instrument that touches your mouth goes through the same five steps before it is used again. The same standard precautions apply to every patient, every time.

    आपके मुँह को छूने वाला हर उपकरण दोबारा इस्तेमाल से पहले इन्हीं पाँच चरणों से गुज़रता है। हर मरीज़ के लिए, हर बार, एक जैसी सावधानियाँ।

    1. 1

      Safe collection & transport

      Used instruments go straight into a closed, puncture-proof container and are carried to the processing area — never left on the chair-side tray.

      इस्तेमाल किए गए उपकरण तुरंत एक बंद, मज़बूत कंटेनर में रखे जाते हैं और प्रोसेसिंग क्षेत्र में ले जाए जाते हैं।

    2. 2

      Thorough cleaning

      Every instrument is cleaned to remove blood, saliva and debris, because residue can shield germs from the next step.

      हर उपकरण से खून, लार और गंदगी को अच्छी तरह साफ़ किया जाता है, क्योंकि बची हुई गंदगी कीटाणुओं को ढक सकती है।

    3. 3

      Drying, inspection & packaging

      Instruments are dried, checked for damage or wear, and sealed in pouches so they stay protected until use.

      उपकरणों को सुखाकर, टूट-फूट के लिए जाँचकर, पाउच में सील किया जाता है ताकि वे उपयोग तक सुरक्षित रहें।

    4. 4

      Sterilization suited to the instrument

      Instruments that enter tissue or touch bone are sterilized by heat. Chemical disinfectants support cleaning and are used for items that cannot take heat — they are not a substitute for sterilization.

      जो उपकरण ऊतक या हड्डी को छूते हैं, उन्हें गर्मी (हीट) से स्टरलाइज़ किया जाता है। केमिकल डिसइन्फेक्टेंट सफ़ाई में मदद करते हैं और केवल उन चीज़ों के लिए हैं जो गर्मी सह नहीं सकतीं — यह स्टरलाइज़ेशन का विकल्प नहीं है।

    5. 5

      Monitoring, storage & checks before reuse

      Each cycle is monitored, packs are stored clean and dry, and every pouch is checked for an intact seal and indicator before it is opened for you.

      हर साइकिल की निगरानी होती है, पैक साफ़ और सूखी जगह रखे जाते हैं, और आपके सामने खोलने से पहले हर पाउच की सील और इंडिकेटर जाँचे जाते हैं।

    Where chemical disinfection fits

    Chemical disinfectants are used to support cleaning and for items that cannot tolerate heat. They disinfect — they do not sterilize. Soaking never replaces heat sterilization for instruments that need it.

    केमिकल डिसइन्फेक्टेंट सफ़ाई में सहायक हैं और उन चीज़ों के लिए हैं जो गर्मी सह नहीं सकतीं। ये कीटाणुरहित करते हैं, स्टरलाइज़ नहीं। भिगोना कभी भी हीट स्टरलाइज़ेशन की जगह नहीं लेता।

    Viral-marker testing

    Where clinically indicated, viral-marker testing may help identify infections and support appropriate medical assessment and treatment planning. Testing is explained to patients and performed with informed consent and confidentiality.

    Test results never change how carefully we work: standard infection-control precautions apply to every patient, regardless of results.

    जहाँ चिकित्सकीय रूप से ज़रूरी हो, वायरल-मार्कर जाँच संक्रमण की पहचान और सही उपचार योजना में मदद कर सकती है। जाँच मरीज़ को समझाकर, सहमति और गोपनीयता के साथ की जाती है। जाँच का नतीजा चाहे जो हो, हर मरीज़ के लिए संक्रमण-नियंत्रण की सावधानियाँ समान रहती हैं।

    • Fresh gloves and mask for every patient
    • Single-use needles, syringes and suction tips
    • Chair and surfaces wiped down between patients
    • Sealed pouches opened in front of you

    Frequently Asked Questions
    
      
    Common questions about DENTZEN Dental Hospital, our treatments and visiting us.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Where is DENTZEN Dental Hospital located?
    We are on Bhootnath Road, Kankarbagh, Patna (ground floor, right of Domino's, opposite Bhootnath Mandir). See the map in the Contact section below for directions.
    
      
    What are the clinic timings?
    We are open 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM six days a week (Sunday to Wednesday, Friday and Saturday). On Thursday we see patients by prior appointment only. For emergencies, please call or WhatsApp us.
    
      
    What is the consultation fee?
    The consultation fee is ₹250. After examining you, we explain your treatment options and give you a clear cost estimate before any treatment begins.
    
      
    Is root canal treatment painful?
    Modern root canal treatment is done under local anaesthesia and feels much like getting a filling. Most patients feel relief because the treatment removes the infection that was causing the pain. Mild soreness for a day or two afterwards is normal.
    
      
    How much does a root canal or dental implant cost?
    The cost depends on the tooth, its condition, the number of visits and the type of crown or implant chosen. After the examination (and X-ray if needed) we give you a clear, written estimate so you can decide with no surprises.
    
      
    Do you treat children?
    Yes. We see children of all ages for check-ups, fluoride and sealants, fillings and baby-tooth treatments, in a calm and friendly way. We recommend the first visit by your child's first birthday.
    
      
    Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
    Booking an appointment online, by phone or on WhatsApp helps us keep your waiting time short. If you have severe pain or swelling, contact us and we will try to see you as early as possible.
    
      
    How often should I visit the dentist?
    Every 6 months for a check-up and cleaning is ideal for most people. Regular visits catch small problems early, when treatment is simpler and more affordable.
    
      
    Is teeth whitening safe?
    Yes, when it is done or supervised by a dentist. We first make sure your teeth and gums are healthy, protect your gums during treatment and use controlled whitening gels. Some patients feel mild, temporary sensitivity.
    
      
    Do you offer braces for adults?
    Yes. Teeth can be straightened at any age. Adults often prefer tooth-coloured ceramic braces or near-invisible clear aligners. We will assess your teeth and suggest the best option.
